Exam format

  • 90 min + 10 min planning. Closed book, paper. Worth 35%.
  • Part A: 12 multiple-choice questions (1 mark each), a mix of definitions and applying them to an example.
  • Part B: a short fictional case on a real brand's ad (picture, target market, media placement), then short answers on message, creative and media strategy. Sample was 23 marks.
  • One question asks you to judge the ad (overall effectiveness, or message / creative / media strategy, or whether it is creative) and justify it with case facts.

Timing plan

  • Planning time: read the case, underline target market, vehicle, objective, what the ad shows.
  • Part A: about 15 min.
  • Part B: about 1 min per mark (23 marks → about 60–70 min), leave 5 min to check.

Common question types in Part B

  • Which execution technique? (imagery, humour, slice of life, demonstration, testimonial, dramatisation…)
  • Which message strategy? (USP, brand image, inherent drama, positioning)
  • Medium vs vehicle; does the vehicle suit the target on reach and frequency?
  • Which scheduling method suits? (continuity, flighting, pulsing)
  • Is it creative? Academic vs agency/industry definition, plus one advantage of creativity.
